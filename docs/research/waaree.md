## Press background 📰 (do not print without confirming)
- India’s largest solar module maker; listed on NSE/BSE after an IPO in Oct 2024 (₹4,321 cr, subscribed ~76×).
- Expansion targets: ~26 GW modules and 16 GW cells by FY27; 10 GW integrated ingot-wafer plant in Nagpur.
- Inverter plant (~3 GW, expanding); transformers via Waaree Transpower.
- Battery storage: plans of 3.5 GWh rising to 16–20 GWh lithium-ion cells and packs.
- Electrolysers: 300 MW, planned to rise to 1 GW.
- US: 1.6 GW module plant in Texas, expanding to 3.2 GW.
- Strong growth through FY26; EPC revenue up sharply in Q3 FY26.
