# CMS home: Pages

**Route:** `/cms` (also `/cms/pages`)
**Permission needed:** `pages.view`
**Feature:** `src/features/pages`, `PagesScreen` (see [pages.md](../../../../features/pages/pages.md))

Every page in one filterable list (status, menu item, search), plus a "Website menu" view showing the menu as an outline. A one-line notice shows what's waiting on you. Click a page to open the full-screen editor.
